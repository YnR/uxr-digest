## Focus Area 2: Research for AI-Powered Products

**AI in Product Teams: In 2026, the Growing Impact on Collaboration** — [UX Collective](https://uxdesign.cc/ai-in-product-teams-in-2026-the-growing-impact-on-collaboration-8ea9f6024dca) *(Sep 2026)*

Anna Lefour reports on shifting dynamics within product teams as AI moves from individual productivity boost to a force reshaping how design, engineering, and product roles interact. Drawing on the AI in Design survey, the piece finds that the share of respondents describing roles and ownership as "messier than before" is rising, and those reporting *decreased* collaboration quadrupled year-on-year from 5% to 20% — yet the number reporting significant *positive* impact on how teams work together grew sixfold over two years. For UX researchers, the practical takeaway is that the organisational context in which research is commissioned, prioritised, and acted upon is itself in flux: studies evaluating AI-powered products should account for the fact that the teams building and interpreting that research may themselves be navigating rapid changes to who owns which decisions.
