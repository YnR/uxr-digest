## Focus Area 1: AI in the Research Practice

**[How to Build an LLM Grader From a Rubric](https://saeidehbakhshi.substack.com/p/how-to-build-an-llm-grader-from-a) — Research toolbox (Nov 2, 2026)**
Part 8 of Saeideh Bakhshi's AI Evals series, this post walks step by step through constructing and testing an LLM-based grader starting from an existing evaluation rubric, using a customer-support response scenario as the worked example. Bakhshi provides a complete grader prompt, a set of test cases with expected scores, and a downloadable exercise kit so readers can replicate the approach on their own data. The practical payoff for research teams is a repeatable method for automating quality assessment of AI-generated content at scale — without having to build an eval framework from scratch.
