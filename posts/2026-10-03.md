## Focus Area 2: Research for AI-Powered Products

**[The New Big Ball of Mud: Why Agentic AI Systems Turn Fragile](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/big-ball-of-mud-ai/) — Tanner Kohler, Nielsen Norman Group (Oct 2, 2026)**
NN/g names a structural problem that should be on every researcher's radar when evaluating agentic products: because AI dramatically lowers the cost of building, non-engineers are assembling complex agent pipelines ("vibe architects") without the design discipline that keeps systems comprehensible. The result is a new category of "big ball of mud" — systems that work in the short term but accumulate fragility that users and builders alike can no longer reason about. For researchers studying agentic AI products, this reframes the evaluation brief: it's not enough to test whether the agent completes tasks today; the research question becomes how users will diagnose, direct, and trust a system whose internal logic may be opaque even to its creator. Mental models, error recovery, and legibility of agent state all become first-order research concerns.
