## Focus Area 2: Research for AI-Powered Products

**Your first draft now has a log** — [UX Collective](https://uxdesign.cc/your-first-draft-now-has-a-log-ee378eb7b3e9) *(Sep 10, 2026)*

Pedro A. Brêtas examines a significant but under-discussed shift in AI product design: when users draft messages, emails, or decisions inside AI tools, the system now captures the raw, unedited version of their thinking before it is refined — a form of data collection that extends well beyond the final submitted text. The piece cites Teramind's 2026 AI governance platform, which enables prompt and response logging across major AI assistants, as a signal that organisations are quietly building surveillance infrastructure around exactly this unguarded cognitive layer. For researchers studying AI-powered products, this raises an urgent consent and disclosure question: participants who agree to have their AI-assisted outputs studied may not realise that their abandoned drafts, anger before it becomes a measured reply, or doubt before it hardens into a decision, are also part of the data. Brêtas frames this as a design ethics problem first — teams shipping AI writing tools need to treat draft-log policy as a first-class research and design concern, not a legal footnote.
