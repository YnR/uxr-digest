## 1. AI in the Research Practice

**[Bottle Your Judgment and Make It Outlive You — UX Collective (Aug 2026)](https://uxdesign.cc/bottle-your-judgment-and-make-it-outlive-you-14f422772262)**
Dan Maccarone argues that the most underexploited AI use case for practitioners isn't speed or scale — it's institutional memory. The premise: hard-won professional judgment (which call to make and why) lives in exactly one skull and disappears when someone leaves. AI can capture and codify that judgment — surfacing it on demand to teammates who never worked alongside the person. For UX researchers, the implication is concrete: the decision logic behind synthesis choices, recruiting tradeoffs, and insight prioritization can be made persistent and teachable rather than dying with team turnover.
