## Focus Area 1: AI in the Research Practice

**[Synthetic Users vs. Human Respondents: Same Conclusion, Less Variation](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/synthetic-users-vs-human-respondents/) — Nielsen Norman Group (Oct 16, 2026)**
NN/g ran a study comparing 120 AI-generated survey respondents with 604 human tech and design professionals on UX terminology preferences. Both datasets pointed to the same directional conclusion, but the synthetic responses clustered far more tightly — producing far fewer distinct answers and less variation overall. The practical takeaway for researchers: AI-generated participants may work for quick directional checks but fall short when you need to surface the full range of user perspectives, minority viewpoints, or edge cases — which are often precisely what qualitative and exploratory research is designed to capture.
