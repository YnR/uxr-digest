## Focus Area 1: AI in the Research Practice

**[Context Libraries: How AI Power Users Manage Context Engineering](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/context-libraries/) — Nielsen Norman Group (Oct 9, 2026)**
NN/g's latest study examines how power users of AI agents — specifically Claude — build and maintain "context libraries": curated collections of markdown files, databases, and live connections that AI can reference and update across sessions. A notable finding is that participants spent more effort engineering these context libraries than crafting individual prompts, and many reported struggling to find their own files over time. The article distils four lessons: context needs ongoing maintenance; letting the agent itself update the library reduces manual upkeep; structure matters for findability; and over-loading agents with irrelevant context hurts performance. For researchers building repeatable AI-assisted workflows, this piece is a practical guide to the behind-the-scenes infrastructure that makes those workflows durable.
