## Focus Area 1: AI in the Research Practice

**Your first draft now has a log** — [UX Collective](https://uxdesign.cc/your-first-draft-now-has-a-log-ee378eb7b3e9) *(Sep 11–12, 2026)*

Pedro A. Brêtas reports on the rapid spread of AI governance platforms — led by tools like Teramind's March 2026 release — that now log every prompt and response sent through ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, and Claude Code inside enterprise environments. For UX researchers using AI tools for synthesis, analysis, or report drafting, this changes the risk calculus: unpolished interpretations of participant data, tentative framings, and raw analytical thinking may now be logged and subject to employer review or legal discovery. A February 2026 Manhattan court ruling reinforced the exposure, finding that conversations with Claude about legal strategy were not privileged because they were "disclosed to a third party, in effect, AI." Researchers working with sensitive participant data or in regulated industries should treat AI tool selection and prompt hygiene as part of their research governance, not just personal workflow preference.
