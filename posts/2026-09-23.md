## Focus Area 3: UX Research Job Market

**[In the age of AI, the UX field survives on leaders who cultivate juniors](https://uxdesign.cc/in-the-age-of-ai-the-ux-field-survives-on-leaders-who-cultivate-juniors-595c548f1d70) — Patrick Neeman, UX Collective (~Sep 22–23, 2026)**
Neeman cites research showing junior UX and design positions declined meaningfully at AI-adopting firms while senior employment held steady — a demand signal consistent with the pattern of AI compressing entry-level work before it compresses expert judgment. The piece frames this not as an inevitable structural shift but as a leadership choice: senior practitioners who stop mentoring juniors accelerate the hollowing-out, while those who invest in bringing up the next tier help the field sustain the pipeline it needs. For UX researchers, the implication is practical: if your org is using AI efficiency gains as a reason to avoid hiring junior researchers, that's worth surfacing as a strategic risk, not just a headcount decision.
