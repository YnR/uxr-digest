## Focus Area 1: AI in the Research Practice

**[Digital Twins Systematically Overestimated Business Software SUS Scores](https://measuringu.com/digital-twins-systematically-overestimated-business-software-sus-scores/) — MeasuringU (Oct 20, 2026)**
Plabst, Sauro, and Lewis replicated their earlier digital-twin SUS study — this time with 289 participants rating six business software products — and found the same bias at nearly double the magnitude: synthetic twin respondents overestimated SUS scores by roughly 10 points on average, compared to 4–6 points in the original AI-chat-product study. The overestimation was statistically significant for all six products. The practical implication is sharp: digital twins built from prior participant data are unsuitable as a direct substitute for new human respondents in usability benchmarking, and teams leaning on synthetic participants to cut costs in quantitative studies should expect inflated scores, not neutral approximations.
