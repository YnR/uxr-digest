## Focus Area 1: AI in the Research Practice

**[The 3 Roles of Context for AI Agents](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/3-agent-context-roles/) — Nielsen Norman Group (Sep 18, 2026)**
NN/g's latest article identifies three distinct types of context that shape what an AI agent can do: global context (persistent preferences and rules that apply across tasks), local context (task-specific information assembled for a particular job), and ambient context (raw ongoing streams such as email or meeting transcripts the agent ingests continuously). The central finding is that curating the right context now matters more than crafting a good prompt — a shift with direct implications for researchers building AI-assisted workflows. Researchers setting up agents for synthesis, stakeholder updates, or literature monitoring would benefit from being deliberate about which of these three context types they're configuring, and which they're leaving unaddressed.
