## Focus Area 1: AI in the Research Practice

**Using AI for UX Work: Study Guide** — [Nielsen Norman Group](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-work-study-guide/) *(Sep 4, 2026)*

NN/g published a structured study guide consolidating their body of work on integrating AI into UX practice — a curated index of articles and videos grouped to answer the practical questions teams are asking: where AI can legitimately accelerate the work, where it introduces risk, and what "good" AI-assisted output looks like. The framing is deliberately measured: the guide opens with the position that AI is not yet ready to take over UX work, but that targeted, critical adoption is now expected of most UX teams. For researchers specifically, the guide provides a map of prior NN/g thinking — from synthesis assistance to AI-powered evaluation — laid out as a learning sequence rather than a list of links. Worth bookmarking as a structured onboarding resource for teams beginning to formalise their AI-in-research workflows.
