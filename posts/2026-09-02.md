## Focus Area 2: Research for AI-Powered Products

**What Minority Report Got Right About AI** — [UX Collective](https://uxdesign.cc/what-minority-report-got-right-about-ai-45b9f1106431) *(Sep 2026)*

Patrick Neeman's latest in his pop-culture-AI series argues that Minority Report's most accurate prediction isn't gesture-controlled interfaces — it's the precog system itself, which generates a fluent, confident account of what will happen and treats it as fact. The plot-critical detail is the "minority report": when one precog dissents, the system buries that alternative reading rather than surfacing the uncertainty. For UX researchers evaluating AI products, this is a precise description of a failure mode to probe: does the system communicate its confidence level, or does it suppress the "minority report" and produce a confident answer regardless? Neeman also notes the labor embedded in model fluency — citing the recent court approval of Anthropic's $1.5 billion copyright settlement — as a reminder that what feels like magic is distilled from specific, traceable human choices that are themselves worth researching.
