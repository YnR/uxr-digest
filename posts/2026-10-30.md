## Focus Area 1: AI in the Research Practice

**[The Time AI Saves Designers Usually Disappears. Here's How to Keep It.](https://uxdesign.cc/the-time-ai-saves-designers-usually-disappears-heres-how-to-keep-it-61eb38e1ed69) — UX Collective (Oct 27, 2026)**
Drawing on the same NN/g survey of 521 UX practitioners covered in the Oct 24 digest, this piece pursues the gap between two numbers that don't add up: practitioners rated AI's effect on task speed at 4.2 out of 5 (well above neutral), yet their overall attitude toward AI at work scored only 3.57 out of 5. The argument is that speed gains in individual tasks get absorbed — by the overhead of prompting and checking output, by the scope expansion that faster execution invites, and by the shifting of effort rather than its elimination. For researchers, the implication is practical: treating AI adoption as a time-management problem (consciously protecting the hours saved rather than reflexively filling them) is what converts speed gains into actual capacity. The piece proposes specific guardrails for keeping the time, including blocking reclaimed hours before they can be reassigned.
