## Focus Area 1: AI in the Research Practice

**[What Working with AI Is Doing to Us](https://uxdesign.cc/what-working-with-ai-is-doing-to-us-c0df05190b66) — UX Collective (Nov 28, 2026)**
UX Collective launched a new experimental essay series examining how AI is changing the people doing research, prototyping, and design — not just the tools they use. The first instalment focuses on psychological and cognitive effects: how working with AI tends to accelerate pace while simultaneously eroding practitioners' tolerance for uncertainty and distancing them from the humans they design for. For researchers, this surfaces a tension between AI's efficiency gains and the slower, messier forms of attention that qualitative research depends on. The series frames this as an ongoing question rather than a verdict, with subsequent essays expected to address different aspects of practice.
