## Focus Area 2: Research for AI-Powered Products

**[The New Siri's Biggest Advantage Isn't Intelligence](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-assistant-context/) — Nielsen Norman Group (Oct 9, 2026)**
Caleb Sponheim uses Apple's new Siri — which entered English beta on September 14, 2026 — as a case study to argue that personal context, not raw model intelligence, is what makes an AI assistant genuinely useful. An assistant that can access the user's own emails, files, and on-screen content can give grounded, relevant answers; a more capable model without that context can only guess. The piece also examines a critical UX research challenge: because context is lost when users move between apps, researchers evaluating AI assistants need to probe not just task success but context continuity and breakdowns. A related design implication is that users must develop an accurate mental model of what the AI can actually access — making mental-model research a key method for teams building or evaluating AI assistant features.
