## Focus Area 1: AI in the Research Practice

**Can AI Detect Usability Problems Like Researchers?** — [MeasuringU](https://measuringu.com/ai-vs-human-usability-problem-analysis-of-a-video/) *(Aug 11)*

Jeff Sauro and colleagues gave ChatGPT and Gemini a recorded usability test video — participants attempting to make a dinner reservation on OpenTable — and asked each AI to do what a human analyst would: identify usability problems. The AIs found roughly half the problems human researchers flagged, and one genuine problem that no human caught. The sobering part: 91% of findings that only the AIs reported required correction or outright dismissal — false alarms, observations untethered to participant behaviour, and outright hallucinations of events that never happened. The practical takeaway is that AI usability review of session recordings can add marginal value as a junior analyst layer, but every AI-generated finding needs expert human review before it enters a report.
