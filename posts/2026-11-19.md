## Focus Area 1: AI in the Research Practice

**[If AI works from anywhere, why are we commuting to watch it?](https://uxdesign.cc/if-ai-works-from-anywhere-why-are-we-commuting-to-watch-it-f903012aaa86) — UX Collective (Nov 18, 2026)**
This piece takes aim at a tension that's quietly building inside research and design teams: AI has taken over much of the execution layer of the work, yet organisations are still investing in physical co-location as though presence equals oversight. The author argues that AI takes the entry-level work from below while remote collaboration handles the mentoring and delivery from above, leaving the value of the commute itself in question. The broader implication for researchers is that the daily practice of research is shifting away from operational doing — running sessions, cleaning data, synthesising transcripts — toward the judgment work that AI cannot substitute: deciding what to study, interpreting ambiguous signals, and knowing when a finding is good enough to act on. Teams that have not yet named that shift explicitly may find their staffing models and onboarding assumptions are quietly out of date.
