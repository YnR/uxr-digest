## Focus Area 1: AI in the Research Practice

**[Do Participants Say Less to AI Than to Human Moderators?](https://measuringu.com/do-participants-say-less-to-ai-than-to-human-moderators/) — MeasuringU, Sep 15, 2026**

MeasuringU ran a controlled experiment comparing their own AI moderator against human moderators, asking whether participants produce less verbal output when speaking to AI. The answer is yes — and by a meaningful margin, not a marginal one. This is the latest installment in their ongoing empirical series on AI moderation, following earlier posts on whether AI can be trusted to moderate and on the mechanics of AI-led usability sessions. The finding matters because several AI interview vendors market the opposite narrative (participants are *more* candid with AI); MeasuringU's approach of building their own experiments rather than relying on vendor-supplied data is a useful model for practitioners evaluating these claims.
