## Focus Area 2: Research for AI-Powered Products

**[Altman and Amodei's "Unsafe at Any Prompt" Moment for AI](https://uxdesign.cc/altman-and-amodeis-unsafe-at-any-prompt-moment-for-ai-6a73f144b56e) — Patrick Neeman, UX Collective (~Sep 23–24, 2026)**
Following Altman and Amodei's appearance at the UN Security Council on AI safety (Sept 22–23), Neeman draws on a striking finding from Stanford's 2026 AI Index: when a false statement is framed as something the user believes, model performance collapses — a sycophancy failure mode with direct implications for anyone evaluating AI products. Researchers designing usability or evaluation studies for AI-powered features need to consider how task framing and participant prompts might inadvertently trigger this pattern, skewing what the model produces in session. The piece also surfaces Anthropic's commitment to embedded third-party evaluators with the right to publish unredacted findings — a structural signal about how independent evaluation of AI products could evolve.
