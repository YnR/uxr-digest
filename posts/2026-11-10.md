## Focus Area 2: Research for AI-Powered Products

**[Usability 101: Introduction to Usability](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/usability-101-introduction-to-usability/) — Nielsen Norman Group (Nov 6, 2026)**
Raluca Budiu's rewrite of Jakob Nielsen's 2012 NN/g classic adds a "Usability and AI" section that updates the foundational definition for the current moment. The section lays out three intertwined problems: AI tools introducing new challenges for evaluating usability, AI agents creating new usability problems for the people who interact with them, and the need to keep usability distinct from an agent's ability to operate a system independently. It also notes AI's experimental uses on the research side—AI-based interviewers, synthetic users, digital twins—while holding that the five components of usability (learnability, efficiency, memorability, errors, satisfaction) remain as central as ever. The rewrite was published November 6 alongside the "Agent Harness: Definition" article covered in the November 7 digest; it did not appear on the articles index until this run.

**[AI Can Generate the Interface. What Makes It Worth Caring About?](https://newsletter.uxdesign.cc/p/ai-can-generate-the-interface-what) — UX Collective (Nov 9, 2026)**
Fabricio Teixeira argues that as AI becomes competent at assembling interfaces from established patterns, the meaningful design question shifts from how the interface looks to what the product should do at a given moment—and that this shift in question is exactly the one research needs to track. Standard usability evaluation, which asks whether users can complete tasks, may miss the layer that now matters most: whether the AI read the context correctly and chose an appropriately-calibrated response. The confetti illustration is pointed—deciding which moment deserves celebration requires knowing user history, intent, and state, not just pattern matching. Teams building AI-driven experiences need research methods that surface contextual suitability, not just task completion rates.
