## Focus Area 3: UX Research Job Market

**[State of the tech job market: late 2026](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/state-of-the-tech-job-market-late) — Lenny's Newsletter (Nov 10, 2026)**
Lenny Rachitsky's latest analysis of tech hiring data confirms that user research has sustained the steepest decline of any tracked product role: approximately 1,800 user researcher hires over the past year against 5,300 in 2022—a drop of roughly two-thirds—with only 108 open UXR roles at tech companies globally as of publication. Google stands out as the sole company to have hired 50 or more user researchers in 2026. The broader picture is uneven: PM openings are up 75% year-over-year, full-stack and AI engineering roles remain active, and mobile and front-end engineering have been roughly halved, suggesting that AI is concentrating hiring gains in a narrower set of generalist-with-AI-skills profiles. For researchers, the data underscores a structural shift rather than a cyclical dip: the report notes that many companies are now allowing non-UXR staff to conduct research themselves, further compressing the rationale for dedicated headcount.
