## Focus Area 2: Research for AI-Powered Products

**[Journey Mapping 101](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/journey-mapping-101/) — Nielsen Norman Group (Nov 13, 2026)**
NN/g's foundational journey mapping guide was significantly updated Thursday to address AI agents as actors in customer journeys — a methodological shift that changes what a journey map needs to capture. The article outlines two approaches: map the person's journey with agent actions included, or map the AI agent itself as the primary actor (with the caveat that agents have no thoughts or emotions to represent). The research guidance is explicit on a critical point: agent activities can be studied through logs and system observation, but a user's feelings and subjective experience must still come from actual user research — inferring emotional states from what an agent did on someone's behalf is not a substitute. For teams building AI-powered products, the update is a concrete reminder that automating a task on a user's behalf does not eliminate the need to understand how that user experiences it.
