## 1. AI in the Research Practice

**[Can We Trust AI to Moderate UX Interviews? — MeasuringU (Aug 4)](https://measuringu.com/can-we-trust-ai-to-moderate-ux-interviews/)**
Jeff Sauro, Lucas Plabst, and Jim Lewis examine whether AI is ready to take over moderation — one of the most judgment-intensive tasks in qualitative research. The article notes that AI is already being deployed as moderator across usability testing, interviews, and contextual inquiry, and surfaces the fact that 419 professionals have signed open letters against AI moderation of qual research. Their core methodological challenge: there is no agreed-upon standard for scoring whether a human moderator did a good job, which makes evaluating AI substitutes doubly hard. A grounded counterweight to vendor enthusiasm.
