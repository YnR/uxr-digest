## Focus Area 1: AI in the Research Practice

**[The AI Reckoning in UX Was 20 Years in the Making](https://uxdesign.cc/the-ai-reckoning-in-ux-was-20-years-in-the-making-ecd7e36185de) — UX Collective (Oct 28, 2026)**
The piece frames the current wave of AI anxiety in UX as a continuation of a concern that has run through the field for at least two decades, rather than something genuinely unprecedented. It marshals current hiring and job-market data alongside a curated reading list to ground the argument historically, and concludes that the skills most at risk are the execution-layer ones that AI can now replicate cheaply, while strategic and contextual work retains its value. For researchers, the historical framing is useful: the profession has survived analogous disruptions before by moving up the value chain, and the current moment is less an existential rupture than an accelerated version of a transition the field has always navigated.
