## Focus Area 2: Research for AI-Powered Products

**AI canvas, Synthetic self, The prompt is the product** — [UX Collective](https://uxdesign.cc/ai-canvas-synthetic-self-the-prompt-is-the-product-801f0da7bea6) *(Sep 6, 2026)*

Fabricio Teixeira's latest UX Collective roundup surfaces three threads relevant to researchers working on or with AI products. The "synthetic self" strand — anchored by Anamol Rajbhandari's piece "Your synthetic self belongs to strangers" — examines the privacy and consent questions that arise when AI systems generate, store, and monetise synthetic representations of users, a live research ethics issue for teams running any AI-backed personalisation. Sophia Hee's "The prompt is the product" tracks what happens when the boundary between user intent and interface dissolves, reporting on her own experience using Claude to build a bespoke tool and identifying where AI misreads goals, over-interprets, or succeeds in ways that reveal new interaction patterns worth studying. Together the two pieces make the case that the artefacts researchers evaluate in AI product studies are increasingly dynamic and identity-laden in ways that standard usability protocols do not yet account for.
