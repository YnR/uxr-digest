## Focus Area 1: AI in the Research Practice

**[Are synthetic users (really) the future of UX research?](https://uxdesign.cc/are-synthetic-users-really-the-future-of-ux-research-fd76fcb479ec) — Arin Bhowmick, UX Collective (Oct 9–12, 2026)**
Bhowmick takes a measured stance on the synthetic-users debate, arguing that human-centered design demands limited use of AI-generated personas — if at all. Drawing on a May 2026 User Interviews survey of 150 researchers, the piece acknowledges synthetic users' main appeal: rapid, logistics-free feedback in early ideation stages. But it cites a 2025 study in which eight UX designers worked with an interactive AI persona called Alice across three activities, and found that the AI-generated feedback diverged meaningfully from what real users produced. The practical guidance for teams: synthetic users can serve as a low-cost first pass for low-stakes artifacts, but should not replace human participants for validation or any research where behavioral nuance matters.
