## Focus Area 1: AI in the Research Practice

**[Context Engineering for Research Synthesis: From Findings to Defensible Conclusions](https://saeidehbakhshi.substack.com/p/context-engineering-for-research) — Research toolbox (Nov 23, 2026)**
Saeideh Bakhshi (quantitative UX research lead at OpenAI) applies context engineering principles to AI-assisted research synthesis, arguing that the context structure provided to a model determines what claims the model can defensibly support. The article draws a distinction between retrieval tasks — where broad context helps — and conclusion-generation tasks, where the context window must be scoped precisely to the specific claim being made. For practitioners, this introduces a methodological step that precedes any AI synthesis work: defining the claim first, then designing the context to match it, rather than feeding all available data and letting the model decide what matters. The piece treats context engineering as a research design skill, not a prompting trick, which reframes where researcher judgment sits in an AI-assisted synthesis workflow.
