## Focus Area 1: AI in the Research Practice

**[UX Roundup: What's UX?](https://jakobnielsenphd.substack.com/p/ux-roundup-20261123) — UX Roundup (Jakob Nielsen) (Nov 23, 2026)**
Jakob Nielsen's weekly roundup opens with an analysis of where AI currently stands on UX work relative to coding: his estimate is that AI trails coding capability by roughly one year, because UX tasks (design quality judgments, usability assessments) are substantially harder to verify than code that either runs or doesn't. The implication for practitioners is that AI assistance will become meaningfully more capable for UX-specific work within a relatively short horizon. The roundup also cites new METR data showing AI coding-agent capabilities are doubling every 3.6 months — a faster rate than the 4.3-month figure METR reported earlier in the year — and covers a Korean study finding measurable time savings from paid AI tools and a preview of Microsoft's enterprise-facing AI agent briefing tool.
